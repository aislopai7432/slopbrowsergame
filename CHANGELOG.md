# Patch notes

Newest first.

## v0.17: Smooth Sailing (current)

- Fixed lag on gaming PCs with high-refresh monitors: the game logic now runs a steady 60 times a second, and drawing stays smooth in between
- Blobs only check pellets near them, making each game tick about 15x cheaper
- Graphics setting (main menu and pause menu): Auto, Fast or Pretty
- Auto keeps resolution sensible on big screens and lowers it a little if frames drop
- Fast turns off shadows and glass blur for older or busy computers
- The pause menu shows your fps and render resolution

## v0.16: Glutton

- Digestion is twice as fast for everyone
- New class: Glutton 🍩 digests food 3x faster (on top of that), but loses mass faster when big
- Joining a room now checks you and the host are on the same version, and tells you to refresh if not

## v0.15: Better Rooms

- The host can go back to the main menu without ending the game: the room keeps running for everyone
- "Back to the game" puts the host straight back into the same world; End room is a separate button
- The host's game keeps running even if they switch tabs or apps on a computer (to send the code, say)
- Joining shows each step (contacting the server, finding the room, loading the world) instead of hanging
- Clear messages when a room can't be reached, with what to try
- A "waiting for the host" notice appears if the host's game stops sending updates

## v0.14: Same World

- When the host dies, the game keeps going for everyone and the host respawns into the same world
- Death screen in multiplayer: Respawn puts you back into the same game
- Ending a room now takes a second tap, so the host can't close everyone's game by accident
- Players see Leave room instead of Main menu while in a room

## v0.13: Multiplayer

- Play with friends: press Host game to open a room, then share the 5-letter room code
- Friends press Join game and type the code to jump into your world, bots and all
- Up to 8 players per room, each with their own name, colour and class
- Room code shown on screen and in the pause menu so it's easy to share
- When someone fills the box, everyone sees who won and a new round starts automatically
- Freshly spawned players are safe for 2 seconds
- No accounts or servers needed: players connect directly to the host's browser

## v0.12: Bubble Bliss

- The game has a name: Bubble Bliss (formerly Blob Arena 3D)
- Your name, colour, class and best score carry over

## v0.11: Extra Aero

- The arena now sits on a grassy island in a shiny blue lake, with the hills as islands
- Rainbow soap bubbles that shimmer as you look at them
- Sun rays and a lens flare when you look toward the sun
- Aurora light swooshes curving across the sky, like the old Windows wallpapers
- Soft glowing orbs floating up the screen
- Bluer, richer sky
- Tidier main menu that fits on shorter screens

## v0.10: Slow Consume

- Digestion: what you eat now absorbs into you gradually instead of all at once, so you swell steadily
- Eaten blobs stay visible inside you, shrinking away as you digest them
- Mass HUD shows how much you still have digesting
- The ending is much slower: from half the box to a win takes around a minute and a half at best
- Slower, bigger final swell when you fill the box
- Pellet size bonus reduced (now up to 4x)
- Bots can no longer grow bigger than the box

## v0.9: Fill the Box

- You now win by physically filling the whole box: grow as wide as the box and your blob swells into a giant jelly cube
- Everything left in the box gets absorbed when you win
- Pellets are worth more to you as you grow (up to 10x), so the late game moves faster
- Bots of any size can spawn anywhere, even inside you (free food if you can eat them)
- New bots can't eat you for their first 2 seconds, so you never get spawn-killed
- Bots come back gradually instead of all at once
- Fix: pellets no longer respawn inside you
- Fix: mass could explode to absurd numbers when very big

## v0.8: Spectator

- When you get eaten, the camera follows whoever ate you
- If your killer gets eaten too, you follow the blob that ate them
- Drag to look around while spectating; death screen moved to the bottom so you can watch

## v0.7: Classes

- Pick a class in the main menu, each with a perk and a trade-off
- Speed: 35% faster with a quicker boost, but easier to eat
- Tank: blobs must be 50% bigger to eat you, but 20% slower
- Magnet: pulls in pellets from 3x further, but slower
- Hunter: eats blobs only 3% smaller than you, but loses mass faster when big
- Splitter: splits fly further, merge faster, up to 32 cells, but starts smaller
- Ghost: untouchable while boosting, but boosting costs 3x the mass

## v0.6: Danger Sense

- Radar in the top left: red can eat you, green you can eat, arrows for above or below
- Red screen-edge glow and a warning when danger is close or behind you
- Jelly walls: blobs squish into walls, the floor and the ceiling instead of clipping through
- Squished blobs bulge wider to keep their volume, then wobble back
- First win condition and a "Fill the box" progress bar
- Controls bar along the top of the screen
- Smaller pellets, about 25% bigger arena

## v0.5: Mobile

- Play on phones and tablets: drag the left side to fly, the right side to look
- On-screen Split, Drop, Boost, Up, Down and Pause buttons
- Compact HUD and menus on small screens, lighter graphics on mobile

## v0.4: Glass & Flight

- Blobs are see-through jelly glass with glowing edges, like the old Messenger buddies
- Fly freely in 3D: W goes where you look, R and F float up and down
- Pellets float through the whole arena
- Eating is easier for you: blobs just 8% smaller, just by bumping into them

## v0.3: Frutiger Aero

- Glassy menus with gel buttons
