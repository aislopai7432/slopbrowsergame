# Patch notes

Newest first.

## v0.9: Fill the Box (current)

- You now win by physically filling the whole box: grow as wide as the box and your blob swells into a giant jelly cube
- Everything left in the box gets absorbed when you win
- Pellets are worth more to you as you grow (up to 10x), so the late game moves faster
- Bots of any size can spawn anywhere, even inside you (free food if you can eat them)
- New bots can't eat you for their first 2 seconds, so you never get spawn-killed
- Bots come back gradually instead of all at once
- Fix: pellets no longer respawn inside you
- Fix: mass could explode to absurd numbers when very big

## v0.8: Spectator

- When you get eaten, the camera follows whoever ate you
- If your killer gets eaten too, you follow the blob that ate them
- Drag to look around while spectating; death screen moved to the bottom so you can watch

## v0.7: Classes

- Pick a class in the main menu, each with a perk and a trade-off
- Speed: 35% faster with a quicker boost, but easier to eat
- Tank: blobs must be 50% bigger to eat you, but 20% slower
- Magnet: pulls in pellets from 3x further, but slower
- Hunter: eats blobs only 3% smaller than you, but loses mass faster when big
- Splitter: splits fly further, merge faster, up to 32 cells, but starts smaller
- Ghost: untouchable while boosting, but boosting costs 3x the mass

## v0.6: Danger Sense

- Radar in the top left: red can eat you, green you can eat, arrows for above or below
- Red screen-edge glow and a warning when danger is close or behind you
- Jelly walls: blobs squish into walls, the floor and the ceiling instead of clipping through
- Squished blobs bulge wider to keep their volume, then wobble back
- First win condition and a "Fill the box" progress bar
- Controls bar along the top of the screen
- Smaller pellets, about 25% bigger arena

## v0.5: Mobile

- Play on phones and tablets: drag the left side to fly, the right side to look
- On-screen Split, Drop, Boost, Up, Down and Pause buttons
- Compact HUD and menus on small screens, lighter graphics on mobile

## v0.4: Glass & Flight

- Blobs are see-through jelly glass with glowing edges, like the old Messenger buddies
- Fly freely in 3D: W goes where you look, R and F float up and down
- Pellets float through the whole arena
- Eating is easier for you: blobs just 8% smaller, just by bumping into them

## v0.3: Frutiger Aero

- Whole new look: blue sky, clouds, green hills, soap bubbles
- Glossy blobs and pellets
- Glassy menus with gel buttons

## v0.2: Split & Menu

- Split (Space), merge back after a few seconds, and drop mass (E)
- Snappier movement, no more jelly wobble when moving
- Easier eating
- Main menu with name, colour and difficulty; pause and game-over screens

## v0.1: First Bite

- Blob Arena 3D: agar.io in 3D with a third-person camera
- Eat pellets and smaller blobs, run from bigger ones
- 24 bots, boost, leaderboard and best score
