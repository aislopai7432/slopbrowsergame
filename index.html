How to play

WASD Fly where the camera looks
R / F Float straight up / down
Mouse Turn camera (click to lock)
Space Split in two, launches half forward
E Drop pellets of your mass (hold)
Shift Speed boost, costs a little mass
Esc / P Pause

Multiplayer: press Host game to open a room and share the 5-letter code. Friends press Join game and type it in. The host's world (and bots) is shared with everyone, and the host needs to keep their tab open.

On a phone: drag the left side of the screen to fly, drag the right side to look around, and use the buttons for split, drop, boost and up/down. Landscape works best.

Goal: grow until you physically fill the whole box, pressing on every wall at once, and you win. The radar (top left) shows red blobs that can eat you and green ones you can eat; ▲▼ means above or below you.

Eat the small pellets to grow. You can eat any blob even slightly smaller than you (8%) by bumping into it. Bots need to be 20% bigger than you. Bigger blobs are slower. Split cells pull back together and merge after a few seconds.
