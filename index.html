BLOB ARENA 3D

Eat. Grow. Don't get eaten.

How to play

WASD Move (relative to camera)
Mouse Turn camera (click to lock)
Space Split in two, launches half forward
E Drop pellets of your mass (hold)
Shift Speed boost, costs a little mass
Esc / P Pause

Eat the small pellets to grow. You can eat any blob that is 20% smaller than you by rolling over its centre. Bigger blobs are slower. Split cells pull back together and merge after a few seconds.
